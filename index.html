About CEdge

CEdge is a portfolio risk analytics platform for systematic investors. It runs daily on live market data, providing factor and sector exposure analysis, Bayesian factor loading estimation, regime detection, VaR/ES backtesting, and decision attribution — helping investors make informed, risk-aware investment decisions.

CEdge is developed and operated by SPES Solutions Inc., a technology company based in Teaneck, New Jersey, founded in October 2024. SPES Solutions builds data and analytics software for investment professionals, alongside educational technology services.
